Roles in ProfilePath

Only the Administrator changes permissions and security settings. The list above is your own access.

Administrator (Head of Guidance)
Has every feature. The only one who opens Security Configuration: the permission table, password and sign-in rules, session timeout and audit log retention. Makes or removes co-admins and exports the Audit Log.
Co-admin (at most 3)
A Guidance Counselor the Administrator chose to share the admin work. Has every feature, including staff accounts, and can read the Audit Log (students are not named and IP addresses are partly hidden). Cannot open Security Configuration, export the Audit Log, or change another co-admin.
Guidance Counselor (licensed)
Works directly with students. By default sees student information and results, writes notes, reviews Monitoring flags, handles counseling requests and retakes, and invites students to counseling.
Guidance Facilitator (non-licensed support)
Supports the counselors. By default View Only or No Access on most features; writes notes and helps with the Question Bank and Assessment Scheduling. Never resolves counseling requests, decides retakes or invites students to counseling.

"By default": the Administrator can give a Counselor or Facilitator more or less access on Security Configuration.
