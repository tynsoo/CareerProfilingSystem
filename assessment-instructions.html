Assessment Instructions

What to expect before you begin the RIASEC Career Interest Assessment.

Before You Begin

This assessment is based on Holland's RIASEC theory and helps identify the careers and work environments that best match your interests. Please read the instructions below before you begin.

Statements

Statements for 6 interest areas: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional.

About 10–15 Minutes

Your progress is saved automatically on this computer and browser, so you can pick up where you left off if you need to step away.

There Are No Right or Wrong Answers

Rate each statement honestly, from Dislike to Enjoy, based on your genuine interest — not what you think sounds best.

Unlocks Your Career Worksheet

Once you finish, you'll see your RIASEC results and be able to start the Career Worksheet.

If your computer shuts down or the connection is lost

  1. Stay in the room and tell your proctor or the Guidance Office right away.
  2. Restart, log in with your own account, and open the assessment again on the same computer and browser. Your answers so far are saved there, and you continue from where you stopped. Using a different computer starts you over.
  3. Your session stays open only until its scheduled end time. If it has ended, or your computer cannot be recovered in time, the Guidance Office will set up a make-up session for you.
  4. Answers count only after you finish and submit. The assessment is taken once, so never let anyone else use your account to continue it.

Once you begin, you'll need to answer every question in order — you can't skip ahead or submit until all of them are complete.
